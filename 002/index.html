Where we are going

Launch. Coach. Exit-ready.

The simplest way to describe what we are building is to follow a franchise brand through its lifecycle. Three macro agents own those three phases.

Stage 01 · Launch
Launch Agent

Gets a brand from "we sell franchises" to "our franchisees are open and operating." FDD signing, broker coordination, attorney workflow, franchise fee collection, entity setup, vendor coordination, POS connection, payroll, insurance, grand opening readiness. This week the signup revamp and starter-trial checkout shipped — the front door of this agent.

Q2 priority · Signup revamp shipped
Stage 02 · Coach
Business Coach Agent

Royalty automation, monthly P&L, brand-wide reporting, AI coaching, performance insights for brand business coaches. Reconciliation data sets (membership + gift card) and the announcements/reporting layer shipped this week. A one-person coaching department can manage an entire system.

Reporting layer live · Formalize Q3
Stage 03 · Exit-Ready
Acquisition Agent

Real-time valuation (Zestimate), exit-readiness scoring, data room generation, lender matching, capital partner coordination, PE introduction. The most defensible long-term moat: by the time a brand is exit-ready we already hold every operational and financial data point.

Q4 2026 · 2027 build
The Q2 sales motion is selling consolidated financials and royalty automation plus the closing and onboarding portals into emerging and growth-stage brands, sold to the brand's finance lead. That is what closes deals this quarter. The Coach and Acquisition agent value is the long-term story that earns expansion and fundraising attention.
Why now matters

Two market signals are lining up at the same time.

Either one would be a reason to build. Together they explain why we are pushing this hard, this fast.

Pillar 01
Services-as-Software is the next category.

The thesis that AI-native services for verticals is the next large category is now widely argued by top firms. Productize services with AI. Capture a services-sized market at software-sized margins. Compound the data on every transaction. That pattern is exactly what we are building, and our CFO offering is the proof.

Pillar 02
The "company brain" for franchise.

The other signal is demand for a system that integrates every back-office tool into one executable knowledge graph. rolluphq is the company brain for franchise. CoverPanda Services is the AI-native services arm. We are building both, and this week's reporting layer is where they meet the customer.

Pillar 03
Our three-entity thesis fits this.

The rolluphq umbrella holds the agents and the data layer. CoverPanda Services is the AI-native back office. B&B Management Co is the founder-led front door. One ICP. One sales motion. Three monetization vectors that compound.

What qualifies a brand as a real opportunity

  1. It fits our ICP (emerging or growth-stage franchisor) and has a finance lead or fractional CFO who feels the reporting pain.
  2. It runs on a POS we integrate with (Mindbody today, Square / Stripe / Toast next) or it is a warm conversation already underway.
  3. It would benefit from at least two product lines (royalty automation, consolidated financials, closing/onboarding portals, CFO services, payments).

The qualified-pipeline figure reflects Product revenue only. CFO services attach layers in once the packaging tiers lock — which is why finalizing them is a priority this week.

Operating rhythm

The number behind the number.

Two ways we keep ourselves honest as the business scales.

Rhythm 01 · Connect the data
QuickBooks + POS into the reporting layer.

The common denominator across Swig, Savory, and Deca is the same: they want automated financial reporting off QuickBooks and their POS feed. Wiring those into the reporting layer is the highest-leverage integration we have — it is what turns a CFO's 15-minutes-per-report into 15 seconds, and it is what makes every partner report we owe generate itself.

That is the work that compounds: connect once, and the investor update, the partner report, and the franchisee scorecard all draw from the same trusted layer.

Rhythm 02 · Measurement
Revenue, retention, the MRR schedule.

The Friday rollup is moving toward a clean accounting of:

  • Gross new ARR added (Product + Services, separately)
  • Expansion ARR from existing customers
  • Joint CFO + Product attach rate
  • Net new ARR and net revenue retention
  • The MRR schedule Avi asked for

Why it matters now: the fundraising conversation in Q4 will turn on exactly these numbers. Building the muscle now means not scrambling later — and it is the same data the partner reports need.
